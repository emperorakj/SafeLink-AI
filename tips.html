🛡️ Stay Safe Online

Simple habits that can protect you from common digital scams.

Think Before You Click

Scammers often use urgency, fear, rewards and impersonation to trick people into giving away money or sensitive information.

⚡
The SafeLink Golden Rule

Stop. Verify. Then act.

🔐

OTP Scams

Scammers may pretend to be bank employees, customer support agents or government officials and ask for your OTP.

🚨 Red Flags

  • Someone asks you to share an OTP.
  • They create urgency or threaten account closure.
  • They claim your OTP is needed for a refund or verification.
🛡️ Stay Safe: Never share an OTP with anyone.
🏦

KYC Scams

Fake messages may claim that your KYC is incomplete and threaten to block your bank account or UPI.

🚨 Red Flags

  • "Update KYC immediately."
  • "Your account will be deactivated today."
  • Links asking for banking or identity details.
🛡️ Stay Safe: Complete KYC only through official bank channels.
💳

UPI Scams

Fraudsters may send fake payment requests or convince you that you need to approve a transaction to receive money.

🚨 Red Flags

  • Unexpected UPI collect requests.
  • Someone asks you to enter your UPI PIN to receive money.
  • Unknown QR codes or payment links.
🛡️ Stay Safe: Remember: you don't need your UPI PIN to receive money.
🔗

Phishing

Phishing messages imitate banks, companies or services to steal passwords, card details or other information.

🚨 Red Flags

  • Suspicious or shortened links.
  • Unexpected login requests.
  • Spelling or domain-name mistakes.
🛡️ Stay Safe: Open the official website or app yourself instead of using the link.
💼

Fake Job Scams

Fake recruiters promise easy work and high salaries, often asking applicants to pay a registration or security fee.

🚨 Red Flags

  • Very high salary for very little work.
  • Immediate selection without a proper interview.
  • Requests for registration or processing fees.
🛡️ Stay Safe: Never pay money just to receive a job offer.
🎁

Lottery & Prize Scams

You may receive messages claiming that you won a lottery, phone, cash prize or gift despite never entering a contest.

🚨 Red Flags

  • You never entered the contest.
  • You are asked to pay a "processing fee."
  • They demand personal or banking information.
🛡️ Stay Safe: An unexpected prize that requires payment is a major warning sign.
💰

Refund Scams

Fraudsters may pretend to process a refund and ask for card details, OTPs or remote access to your device.

🚨 Red Flags

  • Unexpected refund messages.
  • Requests for OTP or card details.
  • Requests to install remote-access applications.
🛡️ Stay Safe: Verify refunds directly through the company's official app or website.
🧠

Social Engineering

Some scams don't rely on technical tricks. Instead, scammers manipulate emotions to make you act without thinking.

🚨 Common Tactics

  • Fear: "Your account will be blocked."
  • Urgency: "Act within 10 minutes."
  • Authority: "I am calling from your bank."
  • Reward: "You have won a prize."
🛡️ Stay Safe: Slow down whenever someone pressures you to act immediately.

✅ Quick Safety Checklist

🔐 Never share OTP, PIN or CVV.
🔗 Don't click unknown links.
💳 Verify unexpected payment requests.
📱 Keep your phone and apps updated.
🔎 Verify the sender before responding.
🧠 Don't make financial decisions under pressure.
